## 1. Backlog Prioritization Framework

Items are prioritized based on operational risk, regulatory compliance, and business criticality:
- **P0: Launch Blockers & Kill-Gates:** Required prior to publishing consumer applications and accepting live financial transactions. Without these, operational failure or financial leakage will occur.
- **P1: Post-Launch Fast-Follows (30–90 Days):** Critical for operational scale, fraud suppression, and reducing manual human review friction as transaction volumes grow.
- **P2: Scale & Enterprise Governance (90+ Days):** Advanced capabilities including automated telematics, predictive maintenance, and corporate credit automation.
