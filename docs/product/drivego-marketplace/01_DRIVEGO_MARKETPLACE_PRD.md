**Document Reference:** `PRD-MKT-01`  
**Version:** 2.0.0-PROD  
**Author:** DriveGo Marketplace Product Management & Architecture Lead  
**Scope:** DriveGo Two-Sided Marketplace (Customer Mobile App, Vendor Fleet Mobile App, Super Admin Web Control Tower, Core Backend APIs)  
**Status:** Approved for Implementation

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Business Mission

DriveGo is an Indian technology-driven self-drive vehicle-sharing and rental marketplace. The platform connects verified commercial vehicle fleet operators and hosts ("Vendors") with retail and corporate consumers ("Customers"). The operational integrity, financial safety, regulatory compliance, and fleet quality are governed by the platform operating team ("Super Admin & Delegated Staff").

### 1.1 Core Value Proposition
- **For Customers:** Transparent booking of inspected, verified vehicles in enabled Indian cities with instant booking confirmation, flexible pickup/doorstep delivery, and upfront security deposit policies.
- **For Vendors:** A digital operations cockpit allowing vehicle fleet onboarding, authentic vehicle photo management, custom operating locations (yards/offices), real-time booking acceptance, automated settlements, and dynamic pricing control.
- **For Marketplace Operators:** A centralised, multi-role Control Tower ensuring strict fiduciary control, Maker-Checker segregation of duties, regulatory data protection (DPDP Act 2025), dispute adjudication, and fleet telemetry monitoring.

## 3. Marketplace Domain Requirements

### 3.1 Vendor Onboarding & Security Deposit Governance
- **Requirement 3.1.1 (One Authoritative Deposit Policy):** The Super Admin sets the platform deposit policy (amount in INR, refund conditions, deadline days, payment provider). The backend stores versioned policies.
- **Requirement 3.1.2 (No Arbitrary Fallbacks):** If no active policy exists, the backend must return an explicit configuration error (`404 NOT_FOUND: No active deposit policy configured`) rather than charging a silent hardcoded fallback (e.g. ₹25,000).
- **Requirement 3.1.3 (Server-Authoritative Orders):** Vendor apps retrieve the exact required amount from `GET /vendors/me/onboarding/deposit` and initiate Razorpay checkout using order credentials generated by `POST /vendors/me/onboarding/deposit/create-order`.
- **Requirement 3.1.4 (Cryptographic Verification):** Payment completion is confirmed only via HMAC SHA256 signature verification in `POST /vendors/me/onboarding/deposit/verify-payment` and webhook ingestion. Client-side success flags are never trusted as proof of payment.
- **Requirement 3.1.5 (Historical Immutability):** Subsequent policy updates increment the version and deactivate the prior policy, but existing paid deposits or active escrow records remain unaltered.

### 3.2 Vehicle Listing, Authentic Photo Pipeline & Lifecycle
- **Requirement 3.2.1 (Authentic Vehicle Photos):** The vendor app must strictly use the native device photo picker (gallery) and camera to capture real photos of the vehicle. All hardcoded sample/stock images (`samplePhotos`) are eliminated.
- **Requirement 3.2.2 (Secure Direct-to-Storage Upload):** Photos are uploaded directly to configured object storage (S3 / Cloudflare R2 / Local dev) via presigned URLs obtained from `POST /uploads/presign` with `fileType: 'car-photo'`.
- **Requirement 3.2.3 (Photo Validation):** A vehicle must have at least 1 real uploaded photo to submit for publication. Empty submissions are rejected client-side and server-side.
- **Requirement 3.2.4 (Explicit Publication Lifecycle):**
  - `DRAFT`: Saved locally or on server, not bookable.
  - `SUBMITTED / PENDING_VERIFICATION`: Awaiting admin review.
  - `VERIFIED`: Compliance approved by admin.
  - `ACTIVE`: Activated by vendor, live on customer search.
  - `SUSPENDED / REJECTED`: Inactive due to compliance or operational hold.
- **Requirement 3.2.5 (Bounded Asynchronous Submission):** The publishing UI must enforce a 15-second bounded timeout, display clear server-side validation error messages on failure, provide a safe retry action, and refresh to the authoritative state without getting stuck in an infinite spinner.

### 3.3 City, Vendor Operating Locations & Pickup Hubs
- **Requirement 3.3.1 (Separation of Geographic Concepts):**
  - *Supported City:* Enabled by Super Admin where DriveGo operates.
  - *Vendor Operating Location:* A physical yard, branch office, or collection point registered by the vendor in an enabled city.
  - *Vehicle Home Location:* The specific vendor operating location where a vehicle is stationed.
  - *Service Area:* Operational boundary polygon for doorstep delivery.
- **Requirement 3.3.2 (No Fictional Preset Hubs):** Eliminate generic presets ("Main Hub", "Airport Service Hub", "Downtown Station"). Vendors select from supported cities and create their genuine operating locations with real addresses, geocoordinates, operating hours, and contact numbers.
- **Requirement 3.3.3 (Vehicle-to-Location Binding):** Every vehicle must be associated with an active, vendor-owned operating location (`pickupHubId`). Vendors cannot associate vehicles with locations owned by another partner.
- **Requirement 3.3.4 (Customer Fulfillment Filtering):** Customer search filters vehicles based on proximity to selected pickup hubs or doorstep delivery coverage within verified polygon boundaries.

### 3.4 Promotions, Offers & Campaign Engine
- **Requirement 3.4.1 (Scope Diversity):** Campaigns must support platform-wide, city-specific, vehicle-specific, vendor-specific, customer-segment, and coupon-code-based triggers.
- **Requirement 3.4.2 (Server-Authoritative Pricing):** Discounts are calculated exclusively by the backend during booking quotation. The mobile client never computes or passes discount amounts.
- **Requirement 3.4.3 (Budget Caps & Concurrent Redemptions):** The engine tracks consumed budget and total redemptions atomically to prevent over-redemption during peak booking concurrency.
- **Requirement 3.4.4 (Financial Attribution):** Every applied discount records whether it is vendor-funded or platform-funded in the double-entry accounting ledger.

## 4. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

1. **Security & RBAC:** Role-based access control enforced at database and API levels for all 7 staff roles. High-value payouts (> ₹50,000) require Maker-Checker dual authorization.
2. **Reliability & Idempotency:** Payment webhooks and financial ledger postings enforce strict idempotency keys (`idempotencyKey`).
3. **Auditability:** Every administrative status modification (vendor approval, vehicle suspension, deposit waiver) writes an immutable entry into `AuditLog`.
4. **Performance:** Vehicle search response time $\le 300$ ms with Redis caching; image uploads execute directly to object storage via presigned PUT.
5. **Data Protection:** Statutory DPDP Act 2025 compliance: customer personal data anonymized upon verified erasure request while preserving financial ledgers for 7 years.
