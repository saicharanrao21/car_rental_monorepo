## 1. Executive Summary & Authoritative Baseline Synthesis

DriveGo's master product baseline has been comprehensively reconciled in accordance with the founder's explicit, non-negotiable business directives:
1. **Partner Model**: **DEALER ONLY**. Exclusive commercial self-drive car rental marketplace for established rental businesses, agencies, and fleet operators.
2. **Vehicle Ownership Model**: **DEALER-OWNED ONLY**. Partners list exclusively commercial vehicles legally titled in the registered business entity or verified sole proprietor name. Zero exceptions for leased, subleased, managed, consignment, or third-party vehicles.
3. **Minimum Fleet Size**: **ONE (1) ELIGIBLE VEHICLE**. Confirmed onboarding eligibility threshold.
4. **Maximum Vehicle Age**: **ADMIN VERIFICATION-BASED**. No universal hardcoded 5-year or 7-year age limit. Individual assessment based on condition, roadworthiness, RC classification, insurance, and Form 38 fitness.
5. **Customer Security Deposit**: **ADMIN-CONFIGURABLE (GLOBAL + DEALER OVERRIDES)**. Admin controls the global default deposit policy and determines launch default. Admin can configure and modify separate deposit policies for individual dealers with field-level inheritance. No fixed 24h, 48h, or 80/20 rule is hardcoded. Confirmed bookings preserve immutable `depositPolicySnapshot`.
6. **DriveGo Commission**: **ADMIN-CONFIGURABLE & NEGOTIATED DEALER AGREEMENTS**. Admin controls global commission rate and determines launch default. Admin can negotiate, modify, and schedule dealer-specific negotiated commission rates. Precedence hierarchy: (1) Active Dealer Negotiated Agreement > (2) Fleet-Volume Tier > (3) City/Category/Trip-type Rule > (4) Global Default Commission. Historical bookings and settled payouts are NEVER recalculated.
7. **Admin Configuration Architecture**: **VERSIONED POLICY RECORDS & FINANCIAL SNAPSHOTS**. Immutable versioned policy models (`DepositPolicyConfig`, `VendorDepositPolicyOverride`, `CommissionPolicyConfig`, `DealerNegotiatedCommissionAgreement`) with maker-checker governance, quote TTL revalidation gates, and audit trails.
8. **Booking Acceptance Model**: **HYBRID (6 CONFIRMED INSTANT CONDITIONS + MANUAL APPROVAL)**. Eligible vehicles and dealers receive instant bookings when all six configured eligibility conditions pass (`FND-CONF-14`). Manual dealer approval is required wherever instant-booking eligibility conditions are not met (`FND-CONF-08`). Manual booking response handling is preserved; dealer response SLA duration and acceptance timeout window remain pending explicit operational definition (`FND-UNRES-01`).
9. **Cancellation & Refund Policy**: **APPROVED 7-TIER SCHEDULE ON RENTAL FARE ONLY**. Authoritative schedule: Before dealer acceptance: 100% | >48h before pickup: 90% | 24–48h before pickup: 75% | 6–24h before pickup: 50% | <6h before pickup: 0% | No-show: 0% | Dealer cancellation: 100% rental fare refund (`FND-CONF-09`). Customer cancellation is strictly distinguished from dealer cancellation. Calculation base is confirmed as RENTAL FARE ONLY (`FND-CONF-12`). Precedence is confirmed: "Before dealer acceptance" takes absolute priority over all time tiers (`FND-CONF-13`). Non-rental-fare components (platform fee, additional services) on dealer cancellation are distinguished and marked for founder/CA review; security deposit is 100% decoupled and governed separately by the active deposit policy (subject to deferred CA/legal review). Whether Doorstep Delivery Fee is included in the approved rental-fare cancellation refund base is an open financial-definition issue (`FND-UNRES-02`). Confirmed bookings preserve immutable policy snapshots. Historical settlements never recalculated.
10. **Launch Geography**: **APPROVED THREE-PHASE TELANGANA ROLLOUT**. Phased multi-city scope across Telangana (`FND-CONF-10`): Phase 1: Hyderabad; Phase 2: Karimnagar, Warangal; Phase 3: Nizamabad, Khammam (`FND-CONF-15`). Zero unapproved launch cities or dates added.
11. **Customer KYC Gate & Vehicle Handover**: **THREE-STAGE WORKFLOW & 7-POINT CHECKLIST**. Stage 1: Customers can browse vehicles and quotes without KYC. Stage 2: Customer KYC must be completed before booking confirmation (`FND-CONF-11`). Stage 3: Mandatory 7-point handover verification checklist executed before vehicle release (`FND-CONF-16`: Customer ID, driving licence, booking confirmation, vehicle condition photographs, existing damage acknowledgement, customer handover acknowledgement, keys & documents record). Technical implementation mechanisms (such as 4 photos, customer OTP, cryptographic OTP, or digital signatures) are mobile engineering options, NOT founder-mandated checks.
